# ⚡ Yieldr

**AI-enabled decentralized asset management.**

Yieldr is a platform where **investors discover top traders & fund managers onchain** while **traders validate performance, raise capital, and scale to fund management** — all powered by AI agents and secured by smart contracts.

## 🎯 The Problem

### For Investors
- **Discovery Problem** — No unified view of who's generating alpha across DeFi protocols
- **Trust Problem** — No way to validate claimed performance or understand what drives alpha

### For Traders  
- **Validation Problem** — No verifiable way to prove your edge to potential investors
- **Edge Preservation** — No incentive to share alpha when it just gets copied for free

---

## 💡 The Solution

Yieldr combines two layers to enable trustless asset management:

| Layer | Description | Status |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| 🧠 **Intelligence Layer** | AI agents that discover top traders, analyze alpha drivers, advise on positions, and monitor portfolios | ✅ Live |
| 🔐 **Trust Layer** | Smart contracts for trustless copy trading, fund management, risk controls, and compensation | 🔜 v1.0 |

---

## ✨ Features

### What Your AI Agent Does

| Capability | Description | Status |
|------------|-------------|--------|
| **Discover** | Find top traders across protocols based on verified on-chain performance | ✅ Now |
| **Analyze** | Parse transactions to explain win rates, styles, risk profiles, alpha drivers | ✅ Now |
| **Advise** | Recommend actions on your positions based on data, not emotion | ✅ Now |
| **Monitor** | Track your positions and alert you when something needs attention | ✅ Now |
| **Learn** | Train from top traders and YOUR preferences to become a personalized copilot | 🔜 Beta |
| **Execute** | Trade and manage funds via smart contracts based on your risk/return goals | 🔜 v1.0 |

### Use Cases

**For Investors**
- Find traders with specific criteria (win rate, drawdown, volume)
- Compare traders on risk-adjusted returns, style, and correlation
- Simulate capital allocation across top performers
- Analyze macro events and their impact on positions

**For Traders**
- Benchmark your performance against top traders
- Analyze patterns in your winners vs losers
- Get advice on underwater positions
- Build verified track record for raising capital

**For LPs**
- Monitor impermanent loss across protocols
- Track concentrated liquidity positions
- Compare yields across pools
- Get hedging recommendations

## 🚀 Quick Start

```
1. Name Your Agent    →    2. Connect Wallet    →    3. Start Chatting
```

1. **Name your agent** — Create emotional ownership with a personalized AI copilot
2. **Connect wallet** — Your agent scans positions across perps, LPs, and tokens
3. **Start chatting** — Get personalized insights from message #1

```
Example prompts:

"Find traders with >60% win rate and <15% max drawdown"
"Compare my win rate to top BTC traders"
"What's my current IL and what's driving it?"
```
